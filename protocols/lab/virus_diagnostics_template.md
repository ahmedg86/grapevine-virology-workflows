# Virus Diagnostics Workflow (Lab Template)

This template provides a general structure for describing laboratory workflows used in plant virus diagnostics. It is intentionally high-level and does not include sensitive or unpublished protocol details.

---

## 1. Purpose

Briefly describe the diagnostic objective, such as detection of plant viruses in grapevine tissues using molecular assays.

---

## 2. Sample Types

- Leaf tissue, cane tissue, or other plant material
- Specify storage conditions (e.g., fresh, frozen, desiccated) in a project-specific document

---

## 3. General Materials (Categories Only)

- Extraction reagents (buffers, kits)
- Nucleic acid purification consumables
- Enzymes and master mixes for PCR/RT-PCR/qPCR
- Standard lab plastics (tubes, tips, plates)

---

## 4. Workflow Overview

1. Sample collection and labeling  
2. Nucleic acid extraction (RNA or DNA)  
3. Quality assessment of extracts (optional)  
4. Setup of PCR/RT-PCR/qPCR reactions  
5. Amplification and detection  
6. Interpretation of results and documentation  

---

## 5. Documentation and Reporting

- Record sample IDs, assay used, and run conditions
- Summarize positive/negative calls and any controls
- Note any deviations from the standard workflow

---

This template is for organizing virus diagnostic procedures at a high level and does not substitute for detailed SOPs maintained locally.
